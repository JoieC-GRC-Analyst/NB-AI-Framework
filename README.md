NB Responsible AI Framework

Overview
The NB Responsible AI Framework (Draft v1.1) is an independent, community-driven resource designed to guide ethical AI adoption, governance, and business process integration in New Brunswick. This framework aims to provide a shared language and a clear standard for practitioners, consultants, and public-sector stakeholders.

Governance Pillars
     Cybersecurity-First Design: Aligned with CyberSecure NB and zero-trust principles.
     Bilingual Parity: Standards for English and French, including validation for Acadian dialects.
     Indigenous Sovereignty: Recognition of First Nations as rights-holders, incorporating OCAP® principles and FPIC requirements.
     Critical Infrastructure: Guidance aligned with national security standards for NB-based operations.
     Human Oversight: Mandatory human-in-the-loop requirements for high-risk deployments.

The 4-Tier Risk Model
     Tier 0 (Prohibited): Outright bans on mass biometric surveillance and social scoring.
     Tier 1 (High Risk): Full oversight for sensitive areas like child welfare and healthcare diagnostics.
     Tier 2 (Limited Risk): Mandatory transparency and audit trails for public-facing chatbots and sentiment tools.
     Tier 3 (Minimal Risk): Voluntary standards for internal efficiency tools.
