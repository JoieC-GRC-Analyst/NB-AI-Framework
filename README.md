# 🚀 NB Responsible AI Framework

### *Practitioner-built · Community-owned · Open Consultation*

### **The Vision**
AI is being integrated into New Brunswick’s sectors at a rapid pace. We face a critical gap: **the lack of a shared, provincial standard for responsible AI deployment.** This framework ensures that when we adopt AI, we do so with security, equity, and the specific needs of New Brunswickers—our bilingual communities, our First Nations, and our SMEs—at the forefront.

### **The Mission: A Business Analyst’s Approach**
I haven’t walked a traditional GRC career path, but I have spent my career deeply immersed in the evolution of technology and business process analysis. As a Business Analyst, my focus is on **identifying gaps, mapping business processes, and building bridges.** I’ve analyzed how AI is integrated in other regions and identified a dangerous gap in our local landscape: we are implementing AI without "Security by Design" from day one. **NB Responsible AI (Draft v1.1)** is my contribution to closing that gap—a resource built on 20 years of IT experience and a firm commitment to protecting our province's digital future.

### **🌍 Alignment with Global Standards**
This framework is informed by the **EU AI Act's risk-based architecture** and mapped against the **SANS AI Security Maturity Model (AISMM)**. By anchoring our framework in these global gold standards, we ensure:
 * **Interoperability:** NB businesses speak the same language as international vendors.
 * **Proportionality:** We avoid "one-size-fits-all" burdens, protecting our SMBs from enterprise-level compliance costs.
 * **Evidence-Based Governance:** We use maturity levels to benchmark progress, not just vague ethics.

### **🛡️ The 4-Tier Risk Model & Maturity Roadmap**
We use a tiered approach to classify risk, combined with a **5-stage SANS maturity roadmap** to guide implementation.
| Tier | Classification | Maturity Target (SANS) |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Prohibited** | N/A (Banned) |
| **1** | **High Risk** | Stage 4 (Integrated) |
| **2** | **Limited Risk** | Stage 3 (Managed) |
| **3** | **Minimal Risk** | Stage 2 (Defined) |

### **💡 Governance Pillars (The "NB Overlay")**
 * **🔐 Cybersecurity-First:** Aligned with *CyberSecure NB* & zero-trust principles.
 * **🗣️ Bilingual by Design:** English & French parity, including Acadian dialect validation.
 * **🪶 Indigenous Partnership:** OCAP® & FPIC aligned; First Nations as rights-holders.
 * **⚡ Critical Infrastructure:** Aligned with national security standards for NB-based operations.
 * **🧑‍⚖️ Human Oversight:** Mandatory human-in-the-loop requirements for Tier 1 deployments.
